I love trying local dishes, getting lost in postcard-worthy landscapes, and chatting with locals wherever I go. I usually start a trip with a rough idea of the beginning and the end — but the more improvised the journey, the happier I am. And if nature is part of the plan, even better.

Traveling this way has taught me to stay open to the unexpected and to see every experience as an opportunity. I bring that same mindset to my work, where I enjoy exploring creative solutions, even when the path isn't the most predictable.
