I'm a huge board game fan, especially if there's strategy, hidden roles, and a chance to sabotage friends with a smile. In video games, I'm m usually the one falling into the void rather than winning — but I love watching how others play.
As a kid, I used to sit next to my brother while he played, totally fascinated by the idea of how those worlds were built. Spoiler: I ended up studying a master's degree in video game development to find out.

That experience boosted not only my teamwork skills but also my love for good design.
