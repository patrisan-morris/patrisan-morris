Dancing gives me life, and not just figuratively. When I dance, the rest of the world disappears — only the music and the emotion of the moment exist.My training at the dance conservatory and my experience with several salsa groups have taught me not only technique, but also discipline and the importance of teamwork.

Dancing fills me with energy, reminds me to enjoy the process, and inspires me to keep learning and maintain discipline, both in and out of work.
