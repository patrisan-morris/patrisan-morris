When a book hooks me, the real world disappears. I tend to lose myself in stories full of mystery, magic, and complex relationships that twist and surprise you. I love that feeling of not being able to put a chapter down because there's always something new to discover.

After rereading Harry Potter more times than I'm willing to admit, I've learned that even the smallest detail can make all the difference. At work, I like to focus on what others might overlook and look for the unexpected twist that makes everything click.
